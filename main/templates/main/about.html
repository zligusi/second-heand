Hey, welcome-this is where we tell you who we are.

We started this company because we were tired of watching good things end up in the trash. We are a second-hand business with a simple mission: give used items a second life, instead of letting overproduction drain our planet's resources."

We are fighting for our planet's future. This is our planet, and protecting it is our shared responsibility. Every item we save from the landfill is one small step toward a cleaner, healthier world for all of us.

We accept items from people, evaluate them, and pay a symbolic price for them — enough to motivate people to bring us their things instead of throwing them away. In return, we promise to find these items a new home, a new life, and a new owner who will actually use them.

We also partner with factories and workplaces. When employees leave a job, they are often given old uniforms and work supplies that nobody really needs anymore. Instead of throwing these away, we work with factories to collect them, compensate them for it, and resell these items to people who need them.

We are a young, fresh company, but we care deeply about the environment and about reducing waste. We are always looking for volunteers who believe in this mission and want to help us build something meaningful, one used item at a time.

Ready to give your things a second life? If you only have one or two items, feel free to drop them off at any of our physical stores — quick, easy, and free. For larger amounts, we also accept parcels by mail, starting from 10 kg. Why the minimum? Shipping costs are the same whether the box is big or small, so by asking for larger parcels, we can keep costs down and put more resources back into our mission — while still covering your delivery costs as a thank you for helping us. Join us, and be part of the change.

Our country is going through difficult times. That's why a portion of our revenue goes directly to charity — supporting people who have lost their homes, and helping protect our country and the people who are suffering because of it. Every purchase you make helps someone in need.
